ARM R -- PRE-REGISTERED READING RULE AND MEASURED CONFOUNDS
Written 2026-09-23, BEFORE arm R was trained and before any arm-R number existed.
Appended verbatim by tools/arm_verdict.py --note; the file is frozen at
out/v04/armR/preregistered_note_R.txt.
====================================================================================================

WHAT ARM R IS.  Identical to arm D in every respect -- GT labels, full fine-tune, the same
KL anchor against the frozen original model on all non-supervised points, the same
rare-class frame resampling, the same optimiser / lr / epochs / batch size, the same config
lineage, and kl_lambda PINNED to arm D's calibrated 1.5305520007241327 -- except that the
supervised points are drawn uniformly at random over the full 360 deg sweep instead of being
the camera-frustum points.  Per frame, arm R supervises exactly the number of points arm D
supervised in THAT frame (verified frame by frame, tools/verify_arm_R.py), and the subset is
fixed across all 10 epochs (verified across processes and across two numpy builds).

    R better than D  -> the frustum's forward-only bias is a handicap
    R worse than D   -> the frustum's spatial contiguity helps
    R equal to D     -> only the COUNT of supervised points matters, geometry does not

----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
CONFOUND 1 -- SUPERVISION VOLUME AFTER VOXELISATION.  MEASURED, NOT CORRECTED.
----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
The two arms' supervision MASKS are matched point for point, but the loss is computed after
GridSample(0.05, mode="train"), which keeps one uniformly chosen representative per occupied
voxel.  A supervised point therefore reaches the loss with probability 1/count(its voxel).
Measured over 96 frames of the real training stream (out/v04/armR/gridsample_survival.json):

    arm D   survival 0.8461      projected epoch total    271,559,132  (audited, actual)
    arm R   survival 0.7078      projected epoch total  ~ 227,176,514  (R/D = 0.8366)

Arm R puts about 16.3 % FEWER points through the loss at identical mask size.  The cause is
itself geometric: arm D's frustum systematically avoids the very dense near-ground rings
directly under the sensor, because they sit below the camera's vertical field of view, so its
points sit in less crowded voxels and survive more often.  This is NOT corrected.  Correcting
it would mean drawing more densely where the cloud is dense, which is no longer a uniform
random subset and would erase the very property the arm exists to measure.

The deficit is NOT uniform across classes.  Projected post-GridSample supervised points,
arm R divided by arm D, same 96 frames:

    person 0.316   large_vehicle 0.520   road 0.559   car 0.604   two_wheeler 0.855
    sidewalk 0.884   vegetation 0.973   terrain 1.033   manmade 1.034

----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
THE READING RULE, FIXED IN ADVANCE
----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
IF ARM R SCORES BELOW ARM D, THAT DEFICIT CANNOT BE ATTRIBUTED TO GEOMETRY UNLESS IT EXCEEDS
WHAT A 16.3 % REDUCTION IN SUPERVISED POINTS COULD PLAUSIBLY PRODUCE.  IF R LANDS BELOW D BY A
SMALL MARGIN, THE HONEST VERDICT IS "CONFOUNDED WITH SUPERVISION VOLUME", NOT "CONTIGUITY
HELPS".

Corollaries, also fixed in advance:

  * The same rule applies per class, and there it bites harder.  A deficit on person, road,
    car or large_vehicle is the LEAST attributable to geometry, because arm R supervises
    0.32x to 0.60x as many points of those classes after voxelisation.  A deficit on terrain,
    vegetation or manmade is the MOST attributable, because arm R supervises as many or more
    points of those classes (0.97x to 1.03x).
  * R ABOVE D is NOT subject to this discount.  Arm R wins, if it wins, while carrying fewer
    supervised points, so a positive result is conservative and may be read directly.
  * R EQUAL TO D is ambiguous in one direction only: equality reached while R carries 16 %
    less supervision is weak evidence that the frustum's shape costs something, not evidence
    that geometry is irrelevant.  Do not quote it as "only the count matters".
  * The decision band is the pre-registered +-0.60 on out-of-frustum mIoU-9, unchanged.
    two_wheeler abstains in all three subsets, unchanged.

----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
CONFOUND 2 -- THE KL ANCHOR COVERS A DIFFERENT SET.  STATED, NOT FIXABLE.
----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
In arm D the anti-forgetting KL applies to the out-of-frustum points, which is EXACTLY the
evaluation region: arm D is anchored precisely where it is scored.  In arm R the
non-supervised points are scattered and interleaved with the supervised ones, so the KL
applies to a differently-shaped set that overlaps the evaluation region only partially, and
to 0.954 percentage points more of the cloud (arm D's frustum also contains label-EXCLUDED
points, which receive neither loss nor anchor; arm R has no such gap).

This asymmetry is a real limitation of the D-vs-R comparison and cannot be removed without
either changing what the KL means or breaking the per-frame count match.  It is recorded here
so that it is not discovered after the number is known.

----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
CLASS DISTRIBUTIONS -- DELIBERATELY NOT EQUALISED
----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Arm D's supervised points carry the frustum's class mix; arm R's carry the full-sweep mix.
That difference IS part of what "geometry" means here and resampling to equalise it would
destroy the comparison.  Supervision-mask level, 64 frames of the real training stream
(out/v04/armR/verify_armR.json), arm D vs arm R, percent:

    car           6.257 / 4.330      large_vehicle 0.286 / 0.103    two_wheeler 0.085 / 0.092
    person        0.043 / 0.065      road         28.966 / 19.535   sidewalk   13.166 / 16.462
    terrain       7.433 / 8.673      vegetation   27.558 / 27.607   manmade    16.205 / 23.133
