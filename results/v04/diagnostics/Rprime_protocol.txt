WHAT ARM R' IS.  Identical to arm D in every respect -- SemanticKITTI GROUND TRUTH labels, full
fine-tune of the released nuScenes PTv3 through the 16-way head with MiB marginalisation, the same
anti-forgetting KL against the frozen original model on all non-supervised points, the same
frame-level rare-class resampling from the same JSON under the same seed, the same optimiser, lr,
10 epochs, batch size 2, the same config lineage, and kl_lambda PINNED to arm D's calibrated
1.5305520007241327 -- except that the supervised set is drawn uniformly at random from the
SURVIVING VOXELS of the full 360 deg sweep instead of being the camera-frustum points.

Per frame, arm R' supervises exactly the number of voxels arm D supervises in THAT frame and THAT
voxelisation, counted AFTER GridSample(0.05, mode="train") -- not the mask size, which is what arm R
matched.  The subset is a deterministic function of a BLAKE2b(salt | sequence | frame) ranking that
depends on nothing else; the selector draws no random number at all.

WHY IT EXISTS.  Arm R matches arm D at the MASK level and then loses the match in the voxeliser.
Measured over 96 frames of the real training stream (out/v04/armR/gridsample_survival.json):
frustum points survive GridSample at 0.8461, uniformly-random points at 0.7078, so arm R feeds
16.3 % FEWER points into the loss than arm D at identical mask size, and the deficit is wildly
uneven across classes (post-grid R/D: person 0.316, large_vehicle 0.520, road 0.559, car 0.604,
two_wheeler 0.855, sidewalk 0.884, vegetation 0.973, terrain 1.033, manmade 1.034).  A drop on
person or road in arm R is therefore confounded with arm R having one third the supervision there,
and arm R alone cannot answer "does the frustum's geometry matter".  Arm R' removes exactly that
confound and nothing else.

----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
WHAT R' vs D MEANS.  FIXED IN ADVANCE.
----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
The two arms spend the SAME post-voxelisation supervision budget, per frame, on GT labels, under the
same anchor strength.  They differ in WHERE that budget is spent: arm D's voxels are the survivors
of the camera frustum -- contiguous, forward-only, azimuthally narrow, range-dependent, and
systematically avoiding the dense near-ground rings under the sensor, which lie below the camera's
vertical field of view -- while arm R''s are scattered uniformly over the whole sweep.  A difference
between them is therefore attributable to the GEOMETRY of the supervised set, and NOT to how much
supervision reached the loss.

    R' ABOVE D   the frustum's shape is a handicap: the same budget spent uniformly teaches the
                 never-supervised region better than the camera's own footprint does.
    R' BELOW D   the frustum's contiguity and its forward concentration actually help.
    R' EQUAL D   at fixed post-voxelisation supervision volume, WHERE the labels sit does not
                 measurably change what propagates to the unsupervised region.

The decision band is the pre-registered +-0.60 on out-of-frustum mIoU-9, unchanged.  two_wheeler
remains an ABSTAIN CELL in all three subsets, unchanged.  Per-class signs are read against their own
3-sigma thresholds from out/v04/armA_thresholds.json, unchanged.

TWO THINGS THIS CONTRAST DOES NOT CONTROL, DECLARED NOW SO THEY ARE NOT DISCOVERED LATER:

  1. THE CLASS MIX STILL DIFFERS, DELIBERATELY (G3).  The frustum's class composition IS part of
     what "geometry" means here, and equalising it would destroy the comparison.  So the honest
     phrasing of any R'-vs-D result is "the geometry of the supervised set, INCLUDING the class mix
     that geometry induces" -- never "geometry at fixed class mix".  Both distributions are in
     PART 2; neither is equalised.

  2. THE KL ANCHOR COVERS A DIFFERENTLY SHAPED SET (G6).  In arm D the anti-forgetting KL applies
     to the out-of-frustum voxels, which is EXACTLY the evaluation region: arm D is anchored
     precisely where it is scored.  In arm R' the non-supervised voxels are scattered and
     interleaved with the supervised ones, so the anchor covers a set that overlaps the evaluation
     region only partially.  The SIZES are nearly equal and are measured in PART 2; the SHAPES
     cannot be made to agree without either changing what the KL means or breaking the per-frame
     count match, so this is stated, not corrected.
     THE SIGN OF ITS EFFECT ON THE PRIMARY METRIC IS NOT MEASURED AND IS NOT PREDICTED HERE.  The
     anchor pulls the student toward a model that scores 65.01 out-of-frustum while arm D scores
     74.68, so it is not obvious whether anchoring the evaluation region helps arm D or holds it
     back, and this experiment contains no arm that separates the two.  Consequently NEITHER
     direction of the R'-vs-D result may be discounted or inflated by appeal to this asymmetry
     without new evidence.  Naming it afterwards to explain away an unwelcome sign is exactly the
     move this paragraph exists to forbid.

----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
WHAT R vs R' WILL MEAN.  FIXED IN ADVANCE.
----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Arm R and arm R' draw uniformly at random over the full sweep, both on GT, both full fine-tune, both
with kl_lambda pinned to arm D's value, same resampling, same schedule.  They differ in ONE thing:
the LEVEL at which the draw happens -- arm R draws points before the voxeliser and lets the
voxeliser decide how many survive; arm R' draws voxels after it and therefore lands on arm D's count
exactly.  R' minus R is thus the price of the survival artefact, and nothing else of consequence.

    R' ABOVE R, outside the band   arm R's number understated the uniform-geometry arm.  Arm R's
                                   shortfall against arm D was at least partly the 16.3 % volume
                                   deficit rather than geometry, and arm R's own pre-registered
                                   discount rule ("if R lands below D by a small margin, the honest
                                   verdict is CONFOUNDED WITH SUPERVISION VOLUME") was right to
                                   refuse the geometric reading.
    R' EQUAL R, inside the band    the 16.3 % deficit did not measurably move the primary metric.
                                   Arm R's number may then be read as geometry after all, and the
                                   caution in arm R's note was over-conservative.  Say so plainly;
                                   do not quietly keep the discount.
    R' BELOW R, outside the band   not anticipated.  Report it as such.  No explanation is to be
                                   retrofitted, per P3.

CAVEAT, DECLARED NOW.  Arm R and arm R' use DIFFERENT salts and therefore INDEPENDENT draws
("armR-v04-20260923" vs "armRprime-v04-20260923").  A shared salt would have made arm R' a nested
sub-selection of arm R's own ranking and put an undisclosed dependency between the two arms; an
independent draw instead leaves subset-to-subset variation inside the R-minus-R' difference.  With
one run of each arm that component is NOT separable from the effect, in either design.  It is
therefore part of the R-vs-R' error bar and must be named whenever that difference is quoted.

Arm R is still running on machine A at the time this note is frozen.  If arm R's verdict does not
exist when arm R' finishes, the R-vs-R' comparison is DEFERRED, not estimated.

THE INSTRUMENT CHECK, PRE-REGISTERED AS A PASS/FAIL
----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Arm D's RareClassAudit recorded, per epoch, the number of points that actually entered the
supervised loss over the whole 17,228-frame training stream (out/v04/armRprime/armD_rare_class_audit.jsonl):

    epochs 1..10:  271,626,869  271,587,913  271,567,016  271,502,968  271,574,911
                   271,664,419  271,554,951  271,580,661  271,544,559  271,559,132
    mean 271,576,340    sd 44,340    (0.016 % of the mean -- 17k frames average hard)

Arm R' walks a BIT-IDENTICAL frame multiset (same data_list, same RESAMPLE_SEED 20260922, same
rare-class weights), so if G1 holds over the whole stream and not merely over the probe frames, arm
R''s own per-epoch totals must be statistically indistinguishable from these.

    PASS  every one of arm R''s 10 epoch totals lies within mean +- 5 sd, i.e. inside
          [271,354,640 , 271,798,040].
    FAIL  any epoch total outside that interval.  The arm is then NOT a voxel-count match to arm D,
          whatever the probe frames said, and its number must not be compared with arm D's.

This is a sharp instrument, not a formality: arm R's projected epoch total under the same audit is
about 227,176,514, which is roughly one thousand sd below the interval.  A check that can reject arm
R by that margin can reject a broken arm R'.

----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
THE CROSS-MACHINE REPORT, AND EXACTLY HOW MUCH IT IS WORTH
----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Arm R' is the first arm trained on machine B; arms A / B1 / D / B0 / C / R were trained on machine A.
The verdict is therefore reported twice: against machine A's arm A (out-of-frustum mIoU-9 65.0125
over 6 draws, the reference every other arm uses) and against machine B's own arm A
(64.9965 over 3 draws, out/v04/machineB_spread.json).

STATED NOW, BEFORE THE NUMBER EXISTS, SO IT CANNOT BE OVERSOLD AFTERWARDS: the two deltas differ by
a CONSTANT 0.0160 whatever arm R' scores, because both subtract a fixed reference from the same arm
R' mean.  The comparison can therefore only disagree in one way -- if arm R' lands within 0.0160 of
a band edge and the two references put it on opposite sides of the +-0.60 threshold.  That, and only
that, is what "the two deltas disagree" would mean, and it would be reported loudly.  The real
evidence that the machines are interchangeable is the completed calibration -- zero-shot
out-of-frustum mIoU-9 B 64.9965 +-0.1044 vs A 65.0125 +-0.2471, difference -0.0160 against a 0.524
threshold, and point-level label disagreement cross-machine 3.951 % versus within-machine 4.004 %
(A) and 3.946 % (B) -- not this arithmetic.
